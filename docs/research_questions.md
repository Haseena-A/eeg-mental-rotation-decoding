# Research questions

1. Do Muse EEG recordings collected during mental rotation vary, in the published frequency bands, across the three experimental conditions?
2. Are EEG features associated with item difficulty?
3. Are EEG features associated with whether the response was correct?
4. Do EEG features contain information about response time?
5. Does a model trained without a given participant predict that participant's accuracy or response time better than a trivial baseline?
6. Which channels and frequency bands account for any held-out predictive performance?

These questions are about task performance. They are not questions about reconstructing a mental image or reading out a thought.

A negative answer is a valid result. Four dry electrodes and 38 participants may be too little to support a stable prediction.
